AETERNYX carton 3D assets.

Provided:
  aeternyx-carton.mtl      Material definitions (from three-d-stage export).

To be uploaded (site picks them up automatically once present):
  textures/carton_front.png       front panel artwork
  textures/carton_back.png        back panel (ingredients / composition table)
  textures/carton_top.png         top flap
  textures/carton_bottom.png      bottom flap
  textures/carton_side_right.png  right side (AETERNYX vertical text)
  textures/carton_side_left.png   left side (AETERNYX vertical text)
  aeternyx-carton.obj             (optional) exported geometry from
                                  three-d-stage. Without it, we render a
                                  procedural box at the carton dimensions
                                  from the artwork (135 x 84 x 14 mm).

Faces default to the navy "carton_board_edge" colour from the MTL until
their PNGs are uploaded.
